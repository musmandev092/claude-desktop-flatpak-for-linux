Cowork lets Claude work on your files and run long tasks for you. In this Flatpak it works with no setup.

Where Cowork runs

Since October 6, 2026, Anthropic runs new Cowork tasks for Pro and Max plans on its own servers, on every platform (Anthropic's notice). Tasks keep going when you close the app or your laptop.

Use it

  1. Open the Cowork tab.
  2. Connect a folder and give Claude a task.

Your folders stay on your computer. While the app is open, Claude reaches the folders you connected through it and fetches a copy of only the file a task needs.

No local VM in this Flatpak

Earlier versions of this Flatpak bundled QEMU, UEFI firmware and virtiofsd to run Cowork's virtual machine on your PC. Pro and Max plans can no longer start local Cowork tasks, so that stack is gone: the download is smaller, nothing in the Claude app is patched, and the sandbox only gets your GPU (no /dev/kvm). Old COWORK= and COWORK_LOCAL= lines in settings.conf are ignored and can be deleted.

Troubleshooting

  • Claude can't read a folder: keep the app open while the task runs, and make sure the folder is connected in the Cowork tab. The Flatpak can read your home folder.
  • Cowork tab missing: Cowork depends on your plan and on Anthropic's rollout, not on this Flatpak.
